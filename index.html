Frisbee Track is a training load monitoring app built specifically for Ultimate Frisbee — using GPS data collected from elite international competition. Not estimates. Not data borrowed from another sport. The real numbers from real games.

It translates match demands into personalised weekly targets for distance, high-speed running, accelerations, and gym load — then tracks where you are across the week and tells you what to do next. No GPS hardware required.
