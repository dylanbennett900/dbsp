Scheduling

Session order & placement

01
Speed before acceleration, always. Speed session must come earlier in the week than your acceleration session. The order of those two around team training doesn't matter much, but speed first is non-negotiable.
02
No field conditioning on team training days. Speed and acceleration sessions should not fall on the same day as team training. Gym can be on the same day, gym in the AM, training in the PM works well, or run before gym if doing both field and gym.
03
Lower Body Strength goes last in the week. Always complete this session after all your running for the week is done. This protects your speed and field work quality.
04
If you miss a session, shuffle forward. Don't catch up. Skip it and move to your next scheduled session. Priority if you're tight: Lower Body Strength at the end of the week, Full Body Power mid-week.

Getting started

Your first week

The program ramps up through week one. Don't try to jump ahead.

→
Complete all three gym sessions. If you're unsure about a starting weight on any exercise, go lighter than you think. You'll dial it in across the first few weeks.
→
Complete the Speed session only for field conditioning. Leave the Acceleration session for week two. Your body needs to adapt to the speed stimulus before adding acceleration work on top.

Gym sessions

How to execute the gym program

Each gym session is built around complexes, groups of exercises, labelled A, B, C etc. Work through each complex completely before moving to the next.

Example session structure
A1Back Squat, 4 x 5 @ 80%
A2DB Split Squat, 3 x 8 RPE 7-8
A3Nordic Hamstring Curl, 3 x 6
Complete A1, A2, A3 as a superset (or individually) before moving to B
B1Hip Thrust, 4 x 8 @ 75%
B2Copenhagen Plank, 3 x 30s
Complete B1, B2 before moving to C
%
Percentages are % of your 1RM. Your 1RM is calculated automatically in Teambuildr as you log weights. Complete a 5-rep set, and your 1RM is estimated from those reps, it self-corrects over the first few weeks as you train.
RPE
Where no percentage is given, target RPE 7-8. That's working hard but leaving 2-3 reps in the tank. Getting close to failure is expected and required for appropriate strength development, don't be afraid of that.
⊞
Supersets are preferred but not mandatory. If your gym doesn't allow it, do exercises individually, just don't mix exercises from different complexes together. Finish the full A complex before starting B.

Field conditioning

Speed & acceleration sessions

Your two field conditioning sessions target different physical qualities and are sequenced deliberately. Select them directly in Frisbee Track to get the full session layout with drill demos embedded. The section below explains exactly how.

S
Speed session (extensive), always first in the week. Higher volume, lower intensity. Builds your aerobic speed base and top-end running capacity. Do this on fresh legs earlier in the week.
A
Acceleration session (intensive), always after speed. Lower volume, higher intensity. Targets short, sharp acceleration quality. Comes later in the week once the speed stimulus has been absorbed.
💡
Some weeks you'll see suggestions to add speed efforts or high-speed running top-ups at team training. These are optional add-ons, Dylan will advise on these individually as the program progresses.

Competition prep

Tournament weeks

A specific tournament prep week replaces your normal gym loading in the weeks leading into a tournament. Let Dylan know your tournament dates as early as possible so this can be planned ahead.

Tournament prep week

  • Two gym sessions only, both with a power focus
  • Volume reduced, intensity kept high, maintains neural readiness
  • Manages residual fatigue from prior training weeks
  • No field conditioning sessions required
  • Speed efforts at team training may be suggested, Dylan will advise
⚠
Let Dylan know your tournament schedule as early as possible, ideally at the start of your season. This is planned proactively, not reactively.

Niggles & injuries

What to do if something hurts

The goal is always to keep training, with modification if needed. Stopping entirely undoes progress. The priority is finding what you can do, not what you can't.

!
Contact Dylan for any modifications before skipping sessions. There is almost always a way to continue training around a niggle. Don't guess. Get in touch and it gets sorted.
→
Lower Body Strength must always be done last in the week, after all running. This applies whether you're fully healthy or training with modifications. The back squat and RDL session comes after your field work. No exceptions.

What counts as a niggle worth flagging?

If something persists across two or more sessions, or affects your ability to complete a drill on field or an exercise in the gym at normal capacity. Flag it. Normal training soreness dissipates within two days. Soreness during the first few weeks of the program is also expected as your body adapts.
