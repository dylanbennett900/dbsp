About

Built from inside the sport

I'm not a generic sports trainer who learned frisbee from YouTube. I'm an Australian national team athlete who also holds an elite S&C role with one of the country's most demanding professional rugby programmes.

That combination means the programs I build for your team are grounded in the actual movement demands of the sport — explosive pivoting, disc-in-hand acceleration, repeated sprint capacity, and the positional athleticism that ultimate requires.

Every program I write is built around your team's schedule, facilities, and performance goals — not a generic template with your logo on it.
