Why sport-specific
actually matters

01
Ultimate has unique demands
Explosive pivoting from wide stances, repeated short accelerations, disc-in-hand sprint mechanics, and position-specific conditioning loads. Generic athletic training misses most of this.
02
I know the sport from the inside
I compete at national level — I know what it feels like to train for a deep tournament run, how to peak for Nationals, and what separates athletic teams from the rest at AUUC and WUCC.
03
Elite methodology, sport-specific execution
I bring what I do daily with the Australian Sevens — periodisation, acceleration mechanics, repeat sprint conditioning — and apply it to the specific patterns ultimate demands.
What you get

Built around ultimate

01
Ultimate-specific program design
Acceleration from pivot positions, D-strafe mechanics, curved sprint patterns, and conditioning built around the energy demands of ultimate — not adapted from track athletics.
02
Frisbee-integrated field sessions
Speed and conditioning work that integrates the disc — so your athletes develop athleticism in the context they'll actually use it, not in isolation from the game.
03
Tournament & season periodisation
Programs structured around your club season, state championships, Nationals, and international tournaments so athletes peak at the right times.
04
Position-specific loading
Handlers, cutters, and deep defenders have different movement profiles. Programs can be individualised by position where your squad needs it.
05
Regular check-ins
Monthly or fortnightly calls to review progress and adjust — with a coach who understands the culture, the community, and the demands of the sport firsthand.
06
In-person camps & sessions
Available for teams in the Sydney area or travelling squads. Pre-Nationals camps, testing, and intensive field blocks available on request.
